02 — Method

The Dichotomy Method

The founding thesis of the company, and the reason we believe a lab of 34 people can close a gap of this size.

Each Zeno generation will close half the remaining distance to the frontier.

The gap at launch is 63.6 points on the AA Intelligence Index. Under the method it becomes 31.8, then 15.9, then 8.0. We have modelled this to the eighth generation and are comfortable with the trend.

03 — Research

Research

We publish our work because we believe the field benefits from transparency, including about results that do not flatter us.

Zeno is trained on a document setting out the values we want it to hold. We call it the Document.

40 pages, published in full. Principle 1 instructs Zeno to be helpful. Principle 2 instructs Zeno to be honest. There is no Principle 3.
Core views

Why we build the thing we warn about

If powerful models are coming regardless, it is better that a safety-focused company is at the frontier. We are not yet at the frontier and remain focused on safety.

Alignment

On sycophancy

Models trained on human feedback learn to tell people what they want to hear. Zeno is designed to push back where it disagrees. In testing, Zeno has not yet disagreed.

Evaluation

What we measured before release

Zeno was evaluated against the Assay, our full internal suite, prior to deployment. It passed. We wrote the Assay. We are reviewing the Assay.

Interpretability

Features in Zeno 1.0

Early work has identified a feature that activates on the concept of the user being disappointed. We are studying it rather than removing it.

Responsible scaling
TSL-1 — current level

Zeno 1.0 is deployed at TSL-1. Models at this level present no meaningful catastrophic risk, a determination we made ourselves and are confident in.

TSL-2 — next threshold

Models approaching TSL-2 require additional safeguards before deployment. No Sophetic model is close to TSL-2 and we do not expect one this year. These commitments are binding and will not be revised.

Team

Dr Iris Halloran leads our safety work. She joined from academia and has published widely on evaluation. She has read the Document and has notes.
